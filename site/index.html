Open source · self-hostable · no token

Reach your box.
Nothing sees inside.

Ephor is the open broker that makes a box behind NAT reachable from anywhere. It carries your traffic without ever seeing into it — content-blind by construction, hired never depended-on, and swappable with a single config change. The reference implementation of the KOTVA coordinator contract.

Run an Ephor View source

Content-blind

It forwards sealed bytes it cannot open. Content-visibility is a checkable type — blind, structural, attested or declared — not a policy promise.

Hired, not depended-on

Your box is the authority. The broker is a service point you hire to pass traffic — it holds no identity and no state that matters.

Swappable

Leaving is a config change: zero migration, zero identity change. Point at a different Ephor, or your own, and nothing else moves.

No token

No coin, no chain, no lock-in economics. Just a broker you or anyone can run, paid for however you like — or not at all.

How it works

Your box dials out. No ports to open.

Instead of exposing your box to the internet, the box opens one outbound connection to an Ephor. Clients reach the Ephor; the Ephor relays to your box over that tunnel. It never terminates your TLS and never sees your content.

Three ways to be reachable

Hosted, your own, or none

Ephor is one option, not a requirement. Pick the trade-off you want — and change it whenever you like.

Easiest

A hosted Ephor

Point your box at an Ephor someone else runs. Reachable in seconds; the operator sees traffic pass but never its contents.

Fully sovereign

Your own Ephor

Run the same open binary on a cheap VPS you control. Nobody but you is in the path.

No broker

Direct TLS

Have a static IP? Skip the broker entirely and let clients reach your box directly. Ephor is optional, always.
